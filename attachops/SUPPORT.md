# ASHBIZCON AttachOps for Jira — Support

Public support channel:

https://github.com/thanoswasaswagger/ashbizcon-public/issues

When reporting a product problem, include only non-confidential information such as:

- AttachOps version;
- Jira Cloud/browser context;
- whether the problem occurred during inventory, analysis, export, bulk download or Safe Cleanup;
- the selected scope in sanitized form;
- reproduction steps;
- the exact visible error message.

Do not post attachment contents, confidential Jira issue content, private URLs, credentials, access tokens, API tokens, customer data or unsanitized exports in public issues.

## Support scope

Support covers installation/use questions, reproducible product defects, report/export issues, permission/scoping questions, and clarification of product limitations. It does not include general Jira consulting or custom workflow development.

## Security reports

Do not publish sensitive exploit details or credentials in a public issue. Follow the ASHBIZCON security guidance:

https://github.com/thanoswasaswagger/ashbizcon-public/blob/main/SECURITY.md
