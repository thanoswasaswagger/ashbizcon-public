# ASHBIZCON BulkOps for Confluence — Support

Last updated: 6 September 2026

## Support channel

Use the public ASHBIZCON work-item tracker for installation questions, product defects, feature requests, and non-sensitive support requests:

https://github.com/thanoswasaswagger/ashbizcon-public/issues

When filing a request, identify **BulkOps for Confluence** and include the app version, operation ID when available, site URL only (not private page URLs unless necessary and safe), timestamp/timezone, expected versus observed outcome, safe error category/message, and a redacted audit export when useful.

Never post passwords, API tokens, cookies, authorization headers, page bodies, confidential attachments, or other secrets in a public issue.

## Supported scope

ASHBIZCON supports the current Marketplace version of BulkOps for installation, permissions, discovery, preview/preflight, supported bulk operations, durable progress/recovery, history, exports, and saved presets.

BulkOps V1 does not permanently purge Confluence content. Recovery for pages moved to Confluence trash uses Confluence recovery controls.

## Support hours and response targets

Support coverage is planned for at least 8 hours per business day, 5 days per week, based on India Standard Time. These are initial-response targets, not guaranteed resolution times or uptime commitments:

- **P1 — security or data-integrity risk:** 1 business day
- **P2 — major product failure:** 2 business days
- **P3 — standard issue:** 3 business days
- **P4 — guidance / feature request:** 5 business days

Security or suspected unauthorized-mutation reports take priority over normal support traffic.

## Security-sensitive reports

Do not disclose exploitable security details, customer secrets, or sensitive customer data in a public issue. Use the private security/emergency contact published in the Atlassian Marketplace partner profile once the listing is available. If a public issue is needed to establish contact, provide only a minimal non-sensitive summary and request a private channel.
