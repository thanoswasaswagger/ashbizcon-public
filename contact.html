Support

Contact ASHBIZCON

For general bug reports, use the public tracker below. For purchase, refund, security or privacy questions, use a private support channel.

Public support

Open a support request in the ASHBIZCON public GitHub issue tracker. Please include the product name, a clear description of the problem, and non-sensitive screenshots where useful.

Open support request

Security reports

For suspected security issues, avoid posting secrets, credentials, private customer data, or exploit details publicly. Use the repository's published security guidance and contact channel appropriate for the affected product.

Purchase and refund support

Keep order references and purchase details out of public issues. Use the private support contact shown in your receipt or the affected product’s published support policy. Never send full card details, license keys or passwords.

Published product support

LinkOps support: support.ashbizcon@mohitguruji.com. SeatAudit support: support@ashbizcon.com. For another product, check its guide or purchase receipt for the correct private contact.
