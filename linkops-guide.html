LinkOps

Start with a small, reviewed workflow.

This guide describes the core workflow. Available features depend on your installed edition; review release notes before using a new capability.

  1. Choose the Confluence pages or space to scan.
  2. Inspect source links, destination status and scan coverage. External checks contact destination hosts.
  3. For authentication errors, timeouts or rate limits, make a manual check before assuming the link is broken.
  4. Preview repair destinations and affected occurrences, then confirm supported changes.
  5. Review verification and retain the audit. Inspect ambiguous outcomes before retrying a write.

Need help?

Include the product name, visible error, affected feature and non-sensitive reproduction steps. Keep credentials, license keys, private content and order details out of public reports.
