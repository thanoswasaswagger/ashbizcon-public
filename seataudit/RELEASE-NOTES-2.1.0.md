# SeatAudit for Jira 2.1.0

## Release summary

Evidence-first Jira seat audits with premium review and reporting UI.

## Release notes

Initial Marketplace release of SeatAudit for Jira by ASHBIZCON.

- Review Jira-reported account status and direct project-role footprint with explainable findings, evidence coverage, limitations, and administrator verification guidance.
- Premium Overview, Review seats, Projects & roles, Reports, and Settings workspaces.
- Search, filters, sorting, candidate selection, and a detailed evidence inspector.
- Configurable seat-cost scenarios for management review; scenario values are not billing data or guaranteed savings.
- Formula-safe CSV, deterministic JSON, and local HTML management exports.
- Forge-native and read-only with respect to Jira: SeatAudit does not deactivate, delete, suspend, or change Jira access.
- SeatAudit does not invent authoritative last-active dates or billable-seat status where connected Jira APIs cannot prove them.
